Wikifreq

Find the rare words hidden in a random Wikipedia article.

How to play

  • You get 5 guesses per article. Type one word at a time.
  • Rarer words score more, up to 100 points each.
  • Title words, generic words, and words not in the article score nothing.
  • Words from the same family, such as "run" and "running", count once.
  • Hints cost 50 points each, and you get two per article. Hints reveal part of a 100 point word.
  • Case doesn't matter. Spelling does.
